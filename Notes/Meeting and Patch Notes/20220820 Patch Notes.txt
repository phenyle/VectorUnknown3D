ADMIN/SESSION UPDATES:
Admin Login now works for both the 3d and the 2d game
Admins can create Sessions for both 3d/2d Games
Sessions can be limited to specific levels/stages/difficulty

SCORING UPDATES:
Stage4 and Stage2 both have new scoring mechanics.
Stage4:
Scoring is based similarly to how it is featured in the 2d game
 - Except in the 3D game, all 4 cards are unique (opposed to the 2d game where 2 cards are just scalars of 2 others)
Stage4 obstacles now create the puzzle, create the redherring cards and then recalculates the puzzle
looking at every combination of cards and scalars for the the BEST most IDEAL, LEAST steps to solve the puzzle
Each player action is then compared to the score as ideal/actual.
- If the player completes the obstacle perfectly they get 8/8, 6/6, etc or 100%
- every step OVER the ideal then creates some lower percentage 8/14, 6/20, etc.
Every obstacle then displays the score for each obstacle as #/5 stars
At end of the level, every obstacles scores is stored in two ways:
 - totScore: total ideal steps / total actual steps
 - avgScore: average score across each individual puzzles
The end of level portal now has a new pop up showing this total overall score.
 - new record scores will also be annotated on this end of level popup.
Stage2:
Most of everything above with some alterations.
Stage2 score is calculated by number of steps (chosing a matrix and vector) * timeScore
The time scaling is taken as a polynomial, equaling 0 points at 90 seconds.
 - timeScore =  -(time^2 / 90^2) + 1
 - this gives a more forgiving curve if solved quickly (near 100%)

ScoreBoard:
Scoreboard now shows #/5 for stage2, 3, and 4.
Top scores are no longer just "the best run"
Instead, scores are "best in that category"
So, best time, least moves, best score.  All 3 could be from different runs.

DATABASE:
Lots of new things here, epecially with the new scoring system
Records is much more involved keeping track of best scores and times
Stage4 has a lot more fields now to save (for each obstacle):
 - obs#_ans : the vector of the question
 - obs#_a & obs#_b : the 2 cards expected to be used in the answer
 - obs#_c & obs#_d : the 2 red herring cards
 - obs#_x & obs#_y : the 2 scalars expected ( x*a + y*b = ans)
 - obs#_Ideal : the shortest # of steps that can be taken to solve this obstacles
 - obs#_steps : the actual # of steps the player took to solve it
 - obs#_time : the time it took for the player to solve it
 - obs#_attempts : a line by line recording of each step the player took to solve it

Lots of other Misc bug fixes
