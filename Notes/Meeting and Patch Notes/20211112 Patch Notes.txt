------Stage 2-------
All new, sparkly Stage2 is finally done
New Stage 2 has 3 levels of difficulty
Each difficulty has a decreasing number of target ships
(7 for easy(1), 6 for medium(2), 5 for hard(3))
Stage now features a much improved experience with brigher effects
A new UI that displays the current selected Matrix and Vector
A rotating cannon to give an idea of the outcome
A better topdown camera
The ability to cycle through targets by pressing "Q"
An animated cannonball and chase camera
Burning ships
Progress tracking
and most importantly robust dynamic question generation
No two playthroughs are the same

Don't let the sparkly look decieve that it was just a graphics passover
Pretty much the whole level had to be ripped up and put back together
so that the game could generate dynamic ship placements and calculate cannon
angles and keep track of targets.

>>>>>>> CHANGES <<<<<<<<<<<
---- Stage 4 --------
UI QOL fix: added Up/Down buttons next to scalar sliders
Moved the entrance/exit portals closer when appropriate so the player doesn't
have to spend so much time just running to the exit or getting lost.

Grapple Fix: another pass at a fix for this.
This time I've disabled the physics interacting on the player character while
the player is in grapple motion.  By turning the RigidBody to Kinematic it should
remove the player item from physics interaction updates which may be the cause for
many of the WebGL problems.  Only live testing will tell.
